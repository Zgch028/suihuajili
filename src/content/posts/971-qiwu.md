data-type="jpeg" data-w="1080" style="height: auto !important;" src="/images/posts/971-qiwu-body-3.jpg"></section></section><p><span style="font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span src="/images/posts/971-qiwu-body-4.jpg"></section></section><p><span style="font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">赵匡胤叹息的不是珠鞍</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">——</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">珠鞍是精妙的，叹息的是工巧用错了方向。精妙的工巧用在了编珠鞍上</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">——</span></font><font face="宋体"><span Roman"><span leaf="">——</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">习了十四年，成了一种习惯、一种天性。天性是做出来的</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">——</span></font><font face="宋体"><span
