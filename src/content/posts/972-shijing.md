face="宋体"><span leaf="">太祖禁的，不是佛，是</span></font><span leaf="">"</span><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">毁了农器去求福</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">"</span></font><font face="宋体"><span
