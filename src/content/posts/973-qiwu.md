Roman"><span leaf="">——</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">大势不在他这边了。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span New Roman"><span leaf="">——</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">好纸用来写经，官钱用来养僧，心思安在安静上，国事却荒了。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span
