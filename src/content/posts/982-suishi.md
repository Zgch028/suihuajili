src="/images/posts/982-suishi-body-3.jpg"></section><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">那一年春旱，田里裂了口子。太宗分派宫人，往五岳去祈雨。中黄门捧着香，一站一站地走，山高路远，香烧了一茬又一茬。可雨还是迟迟不来。一个种地的老周，蹲在田埂上，手心朝上接了半天，只接到风里的土。他媳妇在灶边叹气，说井也浅了，淘米的水得省着使。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">天人之</span></font><span leaf="">"</span><font face="宋体"><span class="rich_pages wxw-img" data-ratio="0.5592592592592592" data-s="300,640" data-type="png" data-w="1080" type="block" data-imgfileid="100004683" data-aistatus="1" style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">不怪他们。</span></font><span leaf="">982</span><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">润州大水，汉阳军也淹了；黄河在临济、在范济口两处决了口；陕州、阳谷闹蝗；芜湖下了冰雹；宣州一场雪霜，把桑树和庄稼全冻坏了。北阳县的蝗虫飞来时，数万只飞鸟扑下来，竟把蝗虫吃了个干净</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">——</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">一个普通人，这一年抬头看天，低头看地，两边都靠不住。他能做的，不过是把井看紧，把粮省着，在旱得发白的田边，多蹲一会儿。</span></font></span></p><section style="text-align: center;" nodeleaf=""><img class="rich_pages wxw-img"
