style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">一千卷书，要多少只匣子才装得下？</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">书是活的，得护着。</span></font></span></p><section style="text-align: center;" nodeleaf=""><img class="rich_pages wxw-img" data-ratio="0.562037037037037" data-s="300,640" data-type="png" data-w="1080" type="block" data-imgfileid="100005153" data-aistatus="1" src="/images/posts/983-qiwu-body-3.jpg"></section><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">崇文院是当时天下藏书最多的地方，三馆的库房里，正本副本加起来八万多卷。书都放在雕花的木架上，架上再覆一层青绫帕。为什么要盖帕子？防尘，也防虫。到了夏天，五月到八月，管书的人要把库里的书画一函一函搬出来，摊在院里晾晒。这叫曝书。一院子的书晒着太阳，风一吹，纸页哗啦啦响，满院都是旧纸和墨的香气。晒完了，再一函一函收回去，盖好。</span></font></span></p><section style="text-align: center;" nodeleaf=""><img class="rich_pages wxw-img" data-ratio="0.562037037037037" data-s="300,640" data-type="png" data-w="1080" type="block" data-imgfileid="100005160" data-aistatus="1" src="/images/posts/983-qiwu-body-4.jpg"></section><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">你可能会想：书嘛，堆着不就完了，何必这么麻烦。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">可藏书的人不这么看。书是纸做的，纸怕潮、怕虫、怕火。一部书能传下去，靠的不是运气，是一代一代人肯为它搬进搬出、晒了又收。藏，从来不是往那儿一放，是长年累月地照看。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">一只鸟，陪一个皇帝读了一下午的书。这画面，比什么</span></font><span leaf="">"</span><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">文治昌盛</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">"</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">都动人。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">后来太宗把这部书的名字，从《太平总类》改成了《太平御览》</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">——"</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">御览</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">"</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">，就是给皇帝看的。可它真正的分量，不在皇帝看没看，而在那一千卷里，装着多少原本要散掉的书。天下书多，散得也快。战乱一起，火烧水淹，多少典籍说没就没了。是有人把它们一条条抄下来，收进这一部大书里，才让后人还能看见。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">这就是</span></font><span leaf="">"</span><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">匣</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">"</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">的意思。匣子不只是个容器，它是一种决心：这些东西值得留下，我替后世收着。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">我们每个人，其实都有一只匣子。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">有人是书架，上面立着读过和没读的书；有人是手机里的收藏夹，存着一堆</span></font><span leaf="">"</span><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">以后再看</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">"</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">的文章；有人是抽屉，压着旧信、旧照片、孩子小时候的画。我们藏的东西，未必值钱，可它们拼起来，就是我们这个人</span></font><font face="Times New Roman"><span leaf="">——</span></font><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">你藏什么，慢慢就成了什么人。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">只是别忘了太宗那句话：藏起来，是为了读，为了用，为了有一天打开。匣子盖得太久，书会发霉；收藏夹存得太多，就再也不会点开。</span></font></span></p><p><span style="mso-spacerun:'yes';font-family:'Times New Roman';mso-fareast-font-family:宋体;font-size:12.0000pt;"><font face="宋体"><span leaf="">书吏盖好最后一帕青绫，把库门带上。一墙的书，静静地在暗里待着，等下一个夏天，再搬出来晒一晒。</span></font></span></p><p><span
